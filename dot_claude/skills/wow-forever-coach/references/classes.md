# Class Levelling Reference (WoW: Forever)

Researched 2026-11-07, three days after launch (4 Nov 2026). Sources inline. UNVERIFIED = not confirmed on a Forever page.

## Read This First

- Blizzard reworked every talent tree in the Nov 2 notes; the Shaman and Warrior trees only went live in the 4 Nov build. Guides dated before 4 Nov may be stale. ([endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-launch-class-changes), notes verbatim on [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/wow-forever-is-giving-players-a-big-damage-boost-for-launch/), Blizzard dev notes via [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/wow-forever-official-development-notes-and-class-tuning-383704))
- The beta stopped at level 30. All guide advice for 30 to 60 is theory, and Icy Veins says its gear and zone advice stops at 30.
- Talents start at 10, with a new 16-point gold-medal tier. Dual spec at 40 (developer, beta; final level UNVERIFIED). Respec at a class trainer. ([Blizzard deep dive via Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/druid-and-hunter-class-deep-dives-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-383190))
- Legacy talents: 5 points in Well Rested, then Talented gives talent points from level 5 (so follow any order five levels early). Thrill of Adventure 5/5 restores 5% HP and mana per XP kill. ([Wowhead Ret levelling](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/retribution/dps-leveling-tips))
- Training all class spells 1 to 60 costs about 206 to 207g for every class (Nov 2 notes).
- Third-party speed tier lists (Mobalytics, wowfdb, not Wowhead or Icy Veins) rank Hunter first and Warrior last, Paladin and Shaman around A/B. ([Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/tier-list), [wowfdb](https://wowfdb.com/tier-lists/leveling/))

## Who Can Play What

From [endgametools, build 1.60.1.70790](https://endgametools.com/en/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-race-class-combinations) and [Blizzard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24315643):

| Class | Alliance | Horde |
|---|---|---|
| Paladin | Human, Dwarf | Undead (new) |
| Shaman | Dwarf (new) | Orc, Tauren, Troll, Skyborne |
| Hunter | Human (new), Dwarf, Night Elf, Skyborne | Orc, Tauren, Troll, Skyborne |
| Warrior | every race incl. Skyborne | every race incl. Skyborne |

Skyborne needs the Heroic Pack or higher. Every race has 2 active and 2 passive racials ([Wowhead table](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/new-race-class-combinations)). Dwarf Stoneform now cuts Physical damage by 10%; Undead get Touch of the Grave. The Dwarf racial list differs between Blizzard (Mace Specialization) and Wowhead (Artisan's Precision): UNVERIFIED in-game.

## Retribution Paladin

Guides: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/retribution/dps-leveling-tips) (author says a rework update is in progress), [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/retribution-paladin-leveling). Avoid the user guide "Twist of Light Retribution": that talent was removed on Nov 2.

**Changed vs Classic** ([Blizzard deep dive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/blue-tracker/news/us/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin-world-of-warcraft-blizzard-24302099), Nov 2 notes): Holy Strike at 6. Judgement no longer eats your Seal. Seal of Fury (10) is a tank seal. Consecration baseline at 20. Lay on Hands 20 min. Salvation removed. Righteous Fury at 10. New capstone Unsealed Wrath (31 points): melee hits store copies of your Seal (up to 3), switching Seal cashes them in. Vengeance stacks 5 and needs Seal of Command. Still no interrupt.

**Talent order (Wowhead):** Benediction 10-14, Improved Judgement 15-16, Sanctified Judgement 17-19, Seal of Command 20, Pursuit of Justice 21-22, Conviction 23-24, Sacred Arbiter 25, Two-Handed Weapon Spec 26-28, Conviction 29, Vengeance 30-32, Repentance 33, Unyielding Faith 34, Champion of the Light 35-39, Unsealed Wrath 40, then Divine Strength 41-45, Improved Seals 46-50, Divine Intellect 51-55, Precision 56, Holy Conduit 57-58, Reverence 59-60.

**Key spells:** 1 Seal of Righteousness; 4 Judgement, Blessing of Might; 6 Holy Strike, Seal of the Crusader; 8 Hammer of Justice; 20 Consecration, Seal of Command, Exorcism, Flash of Light; 34 Divine Shield; 44 Hammer of Wrath. Skip Seal of Justice, Concentration Aura, Turn Undead, Holy Wrath.

**Rotation:** Crusader Judgement on long fights, then Seal of Command; Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown; Exorcism on undead and demons; Consecration only on packs. From 40: build 2-3 Unsealed Wrath stacks, swap to Righteousness to cash in, swap back. Stun or Repent then Judge (Command Judgement doubles on stunned targets). Heal with leftover mana instead of eating. Blessing of Might until 20, then Kings.

**Class quests:** Redemption at 12. Weapon at 20: Verigan's Fist (Alliance) or Wolfsbane (Horde, new; chain starts at 18 with "Diplomatic Incident" from Danitha Morr in Tirisfal, about 13 quests through Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep and Blackfathom; [item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=267369/wolfsbane)). Both last to about 30 and send you across both continents. Mount quest at 40.

**Weapons (Icy Veins):** Ironforge Greathammer (Hall of Thanes quest) or Axe of Orgrimmar (Ragefire); Deadmines hammers or Hammerbone / Forsaken Greataxe; class weapon at 20; then Manual Crowd Pummeler.

**Verdict:** Wowhead: "one of the smoother and easiest classes to level... You won't be the fastest... very few classes will die less often or spend less time eating food."

## Enhancement Shaman

Guides: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/enhancement/dps-leveling-tips), [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/enhancement-shaman-leveling).

**Live warning:** Blizzard (6 Nov) says Intellect and spell power currently beat Strength for Enhancement; Blizzard "plan[s] to release hotfixes on Tuesday, November 10" so Strength is always the strongest stat ([blue post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2404092/1)). "Enhancement Shaman players who are leveling up will want to collect Strength and Attack Power gear from now on." Icy Veins still ranks Intellect high: ignore that. ([Wowhead news](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/upcoming-pet-changes-for-hunters-and-warlocks-in-wow-forever-upcoming-class-383790))

**Changed vs Classic** ([Blizzard deep dive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/mage-and-shaman-class-deep-dive-for-world-of-warcraft-forever-383305), Nov 2 notes): totems on a 1.0 s GCD, buff totems last 5 min, totem tracker UI. Imbues last 60 min and stack with stones and oils. Windfury and Flametongue Totems are party buffs. Fire Nova is a spell at your Fire totem. Totemic Recall and Call of the Elements at 20. Two-handers only (no dual wield). Stormstrike is a 16-point talent: 150% weapon damage, 6 s cooldown. New Maelstrom Weapon (instant Lightning Bolt at 5 stacks), Rage of the Farseer (31 points), Mental Dexterity (Intellect to AP). Lava Burst was removed (some sites still list it).

**Talent order (Wowhead, 18/25/8):** Thundering Strikes 10-14, Mental Dexterity 15-17, Ancestral Knowledge 18-19, Shamanistic Focus 20, Elemental Weapons 21-23, Anticipation 24, Improved Ghost Wolf 25, Stormstrike 26, Improved Ghost Wolf 27, Flurry 28-29, Improved Stormstrike 30-31, Mental Quickness 32-33, Spirit Weapons 34, Tidal Focus 35-39, Mindfulness 40-42, then Elemental for Fire Nova. Icy Veins agrees on Shamanistic Focus 20 and Improved Stormstrike 30 but uses Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer, which Wowhead skips.

**Key spells:** 12 Fire Nova (after Call of Fire), Purge; 20 Ghost Wolf, Frost Shock, Healing Stream; 26 Magma, Mana Spring; 30 Windfury Weapon, Reincarnation, Grounding; 32 Windfury Totem, Chain Lightning.

**Rotation:** Rockbiter before 30, Windfury after; Lightning Shield up; drop totems. Pull with rank-1 Lightning Bolt, Stormstrike on cooldown, Lightning Bolt at 5 Maelstrom stacks, Earth Shock, auto-attack. Slow pulls: mana is the limit. Heal between pulls.

**Totem quests:** Earth at 4, Fire at 10 (do it straight away), Water at 20 (lots of travel, do over time), Air at 30 (needs a Razorfen Kraul run; rewards Rage of the Storm).

**Weapons (Icy Veins, to 30):** Ironforge Greathammer or Axe of Orgrimmar; Smite's Mighty Hammer (Deadmines) or Forsaken Greataxe; Twilight Maul (Blackfathom), Corpsemaker (Razorfen Kraul), Rage of the Storm. Aim for 3.3 to 3.8 speed.

**Verdict:** Icy Veins: "not quite as fast to level as some of the strongest options due to early Mana concerns... a comfy experience once you have your build set up."

## Beast Mastery Hunter

Guides: [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/beast-mastery/dps-leveling-tips) (has a "Burning Crusade" template leftover heading).

**Changed vs Classic** ([Blizzard deep dive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/druid-and-hunter-class-deep-dives-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-383190), Nov 2 notes): Aimed Shot baseline at 20. Traps usable in combat, all share one 30 s cooldown. Pets scale from your gear; every family has Bite or Claw, Dash or Dive and one unique ability (Bear Swipe hits 3, Wolf Furious Howl). New Fox family. New 16-point talent Summon Hawk. Aimed Shot, Arcane Shot and Serpent Sting buffed. Pathfinding moved to row 3 (both guides still list it early: ignore). Quiver still used.

**Talents:** Deadly Aspects first, rush Summon Hawk (about 25), then straight to Bestial Wrath. No exact per-level table was readable.

**Milestones:** pet quest at 10 (quest name UNVERIFIED), Aspect of the Cheetah 20, Aimed Shot 20, Explosive Trap 34, Feign Death 30, Volley 40.

**Rotation:** Hunter's Mark, send pet, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk on cooldown (keep 2 up), Aimed Shot on cooldown, Auto Shot throughout, Arcane Shot only when mana-capped. Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire for elites. One mob at a time.

**Pets:** Icy Veins says Bear (Swipe holds multiple mobs) and that all families do equal damage; Wowhead says Cat or Raptor have a 1.1x modifier. These conflict: UNVERIFIED. Pets don't learn ranks on their own; tame a beast with the rank roughly every 10 levels. Feed it: happiness still exists.

**Weapons (Icy Veins, to 30):** Laminated Recurve Bow (vendor), Venomstrike (Wailing Caverns), Raptor's End ("Ormer's Revenge", Wetlands, 29). These are Classic names presented as Forever advice.

**Verdict:** Icy Veins: "the strongest levelers". Wowhead: "probably the easiest class to level".

## Warrior

Guides: [Icy Veins Prot](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-leveling), [Wowhead Prot](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/protection/tank-leveling-tips), [Icy Veins Arms](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/arms-warrior-leveling), [Wowhead Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/fury/dps-leveling-tips).

**Both sites level as Protection.** Two-hander until Revenge, then sword and board.

**Changed vs Classic** ([Blizzard deep dive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/priest-and-warrior-class-deep-dives-in-world-of-warcraft-forever-383192), Nov 2 notes): rage from damage taken ignores armour; rage from damage dealt normalised to weapon speed. Tactical Mastery baseline at 14 (keep 10 rage). Victory Rush at 20 (10% heal after a kill). Thunder Clap works in Defensive Stance. Defensive Stance threat 80%. Vanguard talent: Charge in Defensive Stance. Retaliation, Recklessness and Shield Wall no longer share a cooldown.

**Prot talent order (Icy Veins):** Shield Specialization 10-14, Improved Revenge 15-17, Master of Defense 20-21, Last Stand 23, Defiance 24, Vanguard 25, Defiance 29, Focused Rage 30-32, Concussion Blow 33, Defiance 34, Shield Slam 40. Solo only: swap Defiance for Improved Bloodrage.

**Key spells:** 4 Charge, Rend; 6 Thunder Clap; 10 Defensive Stance quest; 12 Overpower, Shield Bash; 14 Revenge; 20 Victory Rush, Cleave; 24 Execute; 28 Shield Wall; 30 Berserker Stance quest; 36 Whirlwind; 40 Plate.

**Rotation (Prot):** Battle Shout, Charge (Battle Stance until Vanguard at 25, then switch), Victory Rush, Demoralizing Shout, Shield Slam, Revenge, Shield Block vs melee, Thunder Clap, Heroic Strike only with spare rage. Stance changes drop rage above 10.

**Whirlwind Axe:** Wowhead's [item page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=6975/whirlwind-axe) lists the "Whirlwind Weapon" quest (level 40, needs 30) as unchanged, seen in a search result only. No Forever guide mentions it. Ravager: UNVERIFIED.

**Verdict:** Wowhead: "objectively the toughest class to level"; "an enormous glow-up once they reach level 16".
